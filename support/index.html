Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?

Open the App and go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account, then confirm. This permanently removes your account and all of your data — library, ratings, reviews, rankings, comments, favorites, and followers. This cannot be undone.

How do I report content?

Tap the ••• (more) menu on any review or comment and choose Report. Pick a reason and submit. You can also Block a user from the same menu, which hides their content from you and yours from them. Reported content is reviewed and may be removed under our community guidelines.

How do I change my display name?

Go to your profile and tap Edit profile, then update your display name (2–30 characters) and save. Display names must follow our community guidelines.

Do I need an account to use Rochi?

No. Rochi works fully anonymously — your tracking lives on your device. Sign in only when you want to back up your library and sync it across devices.
