Delete Your Account

Last updated: June 24, 2026

To delete your Rochi account and all associated data, open the Rochi app and go to:

  1. Open the Rochi app
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone
  4. Tap Delete Account

This permanently deletes your profile, reviews, ratings, rankings, comments, and all tracking data. This action cannot be undone.

Need help?

If you can’t access the app or need assistance deleting your account, email aaron@asaholdings.io.
